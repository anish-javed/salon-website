Our story

Clipper opened in 2005 with a simple idea: every man deserves a great cut in a clean, relaxed place. Today we have 4 branches in Lahore (Gulberg, DHA, Johar Town and Model Town) and offer every hair and skin treatment a man could need.

Our barbers train all year, our tools are sterilised after every customer, and we never rush a cut.
